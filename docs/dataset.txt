The dataset is not publicly distributed in this repository in order to protect the privacy and confidentiality of the research participants.

The data must not be:

* Redistributed without permission
* Published on public websites or repositories
* Used for commercial purposes without authorization
* Combined with other datasets for identification purposes
* Used to attempt to identify individual respondents
* Modified and redistributed as an official version of the research dataset

Any approved access must be used only for the purpose stated in the access request.
